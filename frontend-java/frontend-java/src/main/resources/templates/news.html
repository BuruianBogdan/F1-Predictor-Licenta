Sources, automation & forecast methodology
Selenium reads public articles on the official Formula 1 website every 30 minutes while this application is running. A local LLM writes short summaries; they may contain mistakes. Always check the linked source.
Only explicitly confirmed, race-specific grid penalties are currently used as experimental forecast signals. General opinions and AI summaries do not change probabilities. Signals are capped and dated to prevent future information from entering historical predictions.
